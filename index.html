The Regal Chronicle

122+ Years of European Supremacy

Follow the defining milestones that transformed Madrid Foot-Ball Club into the undisputed Kings of Europe.

March 6, 1902

The Foundation & The Pure White Shirt

Madrid Foot-Ball Club was officially established by Juan Padrós and enthusiastic academicians. The club chose the immaculate, pure all-white strip inspired by London's legendary amateur club, Corinthian FC, establishing a look that would conquer the sporting world.

Milestone: First Spanish Copa title achieved in 1905, followed by four consecutive cup titles.
June 29, 1920

The Royal Crown Bestowed ("Real")

King Alfonso XIII granted the club the royal title "Real" (Royal) in recognition of its athletic distinction and contribution to the capital. The iconic royal crown was placed on top of the Madrid circular crest, forever cementing its identity.

Crest Update: The royal crown and purple castilian sash permanently integrated.
December 14, 1947

The Construction of Estadio Santiago Bernabéu

President Santiago Bernabéu envisioned a cathedral for modern football on Chamartín. Overcoming immense post-war skepticism, the stadium was inaugurated with a capacity exceeding 100,000, establishing the economic foundation to recruit the world's greatest players.

Visionary Leadership: Bernabéu served as president for 35 transformative years.
1955 – 1960

The Golden Quintuple (5 European Cups in a Row)

Real Madrid helped birth the European Cup and promptly dominated it. Led by Alfredo Di Stéfano, Ferenc Puskás, Raymond Kopa, and Paco Gento, the club won the first five consecutive European Cups, culminating in the 7-3 masterpiece against Eintracht Frankfurt at Hampden Park before 127,000 spectators.

Historic Feat: The only club in football history to win five consecutive European Cups.
May 20, 1998

La Séptima: Reclaiming Europe After 32 Years

After a 32-year drought, Real Madrid returned to the pinnacle of Europe. In Amsterdam, against the heavily favored Juventus of Zidane and Del Piero, Predrag Mijatović struck in the 66th minute to deliver the long-awaited 7th European Cup.

Turning Point: Launched the modern era of European dominance (8 UCLs in 26 years).
May 15, 2002

La Novena & Zidane's Volley of the Gods

In Glasgow, Zinedine Zidane produced arguably the single most aesthetic goal ever scored in a football final: a left-footed volley dropped from the Scottish sky directly into the top corner. Young Iker Casillas produced heroics in the dying minutes to seal Madrid's 9th crown.

Centenary Glory: Real Madrid celebrated its 100th anniversary as European Champions.
May 24, 2014

La Décima & Ramos at 92:48 in Lisbon

Trailing 0-1 to rivals Atlético Madrid in the 93rd minute of the final, Sergio Ramos rose into Lisbon lore to head home Luka Modrić's corner at 92:48. Madrid ran riot in extra time (4-1) under Carlo Ancelotti to finally seize the obsessive dream of La Décima.

Legendary Quote: "Hasta el final, vamos Real!" became the immortal rallying cry.
2016 – 2018

The Historic Modern Champions League Three-Peat

Under manager Zinedine Zidane and spearhead Cristiano Ronaldo, Real Madrid broke modern football's greatest curse. They became the only club in the UEFA Champions League era to defend the trophy, winning three consecutive titles in Milan (2016), Cardiff (2017), and Kyiv (2018).

Unrivaled Dynasty: 4 European titles in 5 years (2014, 2016, 2017, 2018).
June 1, 2024

La Decimoquinta (15th) at Wembley & The New Bernabéu

At Wembley Stadium, Dani Carvajal and Vinícius Jr. struck against Borussia Dortmund to capture European Cup number 15. Combined with the grand unveiling of the futuristic, retractable-roof Santiago Bernabéu and the arrival of Kylian Mbappé alongside Jude Bellingham, a new golden dynasty takes the world stage.

Current Epoch: 15 European Cups, 36 La Ligas, leading European football into 2025 and beyond.
